Find a place to begin

The Books page holds complete works. Each book page includes its contents, a content note, a full-manuscript view, and a Markdown download.

The archive includes books and individual sections. Search the full text, filter by type or subject, and sort by title or reading order.

Make the page yours

Use Light / Dark in the header to change the theme. Use A− and A+ in the reader to adjust text size. These preferences are remembered in this browser when storage is available.

Keep your place

Save a book or section with its Save button. Your shelf keeps saved records and a link to your most recently opened section.

Opening a section marks it as your last reading place; the resume link returns to that section, not to an exact sentence or scroll position.

Reading away from the screen

Open the full-book view, then choose Print / Save as PDF. Your browser’s print dialog handles printing and PDF saving. The layout removes navigation and uses black text on white paper.

Your privacy

The site has no account system, analytics scripts, or advertising. Browser cookies hold only reading preferences, saved record IDs, and your last section. There is no cross-device sync. Fonts load from Fontshare and Google; the site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Those providers may receive normal connection information.

You can remove stored preferences and saves using your browser’s site-data settings. If storage is blocked, tools work for the current session only.

Read at your own pace

The memoir includes graphic descriptions of death and trauma. You can stop, skip a section, or come back later. The author’s account is personal experience, not medical advice.
