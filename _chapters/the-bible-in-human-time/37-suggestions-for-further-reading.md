Ehrman, Bart D. Lost Christianity's: The Battles for Scripture and the Faiths We Never Knew — New York: Oxford University Press, 2003

Kugel, James L. How to Read the Bible: A Guide to Scripture, Then and Now — New York: Free Press, 2007

Coogan, Michael D., ed. The New Oxford Annotated Bible with Apocrypha — 5th edition. New York: Oxford University Press, 2018

Hayes, John H., and Carl R. Holladay. Biblical Exegesis: A Beginner's Handbook — 4th edition. Louisville: Westminster John Knox Press, 2007

Magness, Jodi. The Archaeology of the Holy Land: From the Destruction of Solomon's Temple to the Muslim Conquest — Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 2012
