## Primary Sources

**Ancient Near Eastern Texts** – *Ancient Near Eastern Texts Relating to the Old Testament*, edited by James B. Pritchard. 3rd ed. Princeton: Princeton University Press, 1969.

**Context of Scripture**, edited by William W. Hallo and K. Lawson Younger. 3 vols. Leiden: Brill, 1997–2002.

**Biblical Manuscripts and Versions** – *Biblia Hebraica Stuttgartensia*, edited by Karl Elliger and Wilhelm Rudolph. Stuttgart: Deutsche Bibelgesellschaft, 1997.

*Novum Testamentum Graece*, edited by Barbara Aland et al. 28th ed. Stuttgart: Deutsche Bibelgesellschaft, 2012.

**Septuagint**, edited by Alfred Rahlfs and Robert Hanhart. Stuttgart: Deutsche Bibelgesellschaft, 2006.

**Dead Sea Scrolls** – *The Dead Sea Scrolls Study Edition*, edited by Florentino García Martínez and Eibert J.C. Tigchelaar. 2 vols. Leiden: Brill, 1997–1998.

**Early Christian Literature** – *The Nag Hammadi Library in English*, edited by James M. Robinson. 4th ed. Leiden: Brill, 1996.

## Secondary Sources

## Archaeological Studies

- Dever, William G. *What Did the Biblical Writers Know and When Did They Know It?* Grand Rapids: Eerdmans, 2001.
- Finkelstein, Israel, and Neil Asher Silberman. *The Bible Unearthed: Archaeology's New Vision of Ancient Israel and the Origin of Its Sacred Texts.* New York: Free Press, 2001.
- Mazar, Amihai. *Archaeology of the Land of the Bible, 10,000–586 B.C.E.* New York: Doubleday, 1990.

## Biblical Criticism and Introduction

- Collins, John J. *Introduction to the Hebrew Bible*. 2nd ed. Minneapolis: Fortress Press, 2014.
- Ehrman, Bart D. *The New Testament: A Historical Introduction to the Early Christian Writings*. 6th ed. New York: Oxford University Press, 2016.
- Friedman, Richard Elliott. *Who Wrote the Bible?* New York: Harper One, 1997.

## Historical Studies

- Albertz, Rainer. *A History of Israelite Religion in the Old Testament Period.* 2 vols. Louisville: Westminster John Knox Press, 1994.
- Brown, Raymond E. *An Introduction to the New Testament.* New York: Doubleday, 1997.
- Cross, Frank Moore. *From Epic to Canon: History and Literature in Ancient Israel.* Baltimore: Johns Hopkins University Press, 1998.

## Theological Studies

- Brueggemann, Walter. *Theology of the Old Testament: Testimony, Dispute, Advocacy.* Minneapolis: Fortress Press, 1997.
- Dunn, James D.G. *The Theology of Paul the Apostle.* Grand Rapids: Eerdmans, 1998.
- Smith, Mark S. *The Origins of Biblical Monotheism: Israel's Polytheistic Background and the Ugaritic Texts.* New York: Oxford University Press, 2001.

## Canonical Studies

- Barton, John. *Holy Writings, Sacred Text: The Canon in Early Christianity.* Louisville: Westminster John Knox Press, 1997.
- McDonald, Lee Martin. *The Formation of the Christian Biblical Canon.* Nashville: Abingdon Press, 1995.
- Metzger, Bruce M. *The Canon of the New Testament: Its Origin, Development, and Significance.* Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1987.
