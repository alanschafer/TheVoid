## Archaeological Photographs and Site Plans

All archaeological photographs and site plans are courtesy of the Israel Antiquities Authority and the respective excavation directors, as specifically noted in captions. Where required, specific permissions have been obtained and are noted in captions. These images are used under fair use provisions for academic analysis and research purposes.

## Ancient Text Translations

Akkadian and Assyrian texts are adapted from James B. Pritchard, ed., *Ancient Near Eastern Texts Relating to the Old Testament*, 3rd ed. (Princeton: Princeton University Press, 1969), used under fair use for educational purposes.

Ugaritic texts are adapted from Dennis Pardee in William W. Hallo and K. Lawson Younger, eds., *The Context of Scripture* (Leiden: Brill, 1997–2002), used under fair use for educational purposes.

Additional ancient Near Eastern texts are drawn from various published scholarly editions as cited in the notes, used under fair use for academic commentary and analysis.

## Modern Scholarly Works

Brief quotations from copyrighted scholarly works are included under fair use for purposes of criticism, comment, and educational analysis. Complete bibliographic details are provided in the notes and bibliography.

## Maps and Charts

All archaeological timeline charts, chronological tables, and empire maps are original works created by the author, based on published archaeological reports and scholarly consensus. Geographical relationships depicted in maps are derived from historical evidence as cited in the accompanying notes.

## Biblical Texts

Biblical quotations are either from public domain translations or represent the author's own translations from Hebrew and Greek manuscripts. All manuscript readings follow standard critical editions, as noted in the text.
