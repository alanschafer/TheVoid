**Alan Shaffer** is the founder and publisher of **Nexiom Press**, where he serves as editor, staff writer, and web designer. Born in 1991, Shaffer brings a unique perspective to biblical scholarship, combining a lifetime of intensive religious study as a Jehovah's Witness with rigorous academic methodology and archaeological research.

Raised within the Jehovah's Witness tradition, Shaffer spent over three decades engaged in deep scriptural study through the organization's extensive educational programs. His departure from traditional religious frameworks allows him to approach biblical texts with both intimate familiarity and critical distance.

By applying historical-critical methods to materials he studied devotionally his entire life, Shaffer offers insights that resonate with both academic and general audiences. His independent scholarship demonstrates how personal religious experience can inform rather than compromise rigorous biblical analysis.

Shaffer's chronological approach to biblical development synthesizes archaeological evidence, literary analysis, and historical investigation, illuminating how scripture emerged over sixteen centuries of human experience. His work bridges the gap between devotional and critical study, making complex biblical scholarship accessible to readers seeking to understand how the world's most influential book came to be.

*The Bible in Human Time* represents Shaffer's contribution to biblical studies, providing the first comprehensive chronological history of biblical development written for both scholars and general readers who want to understand the human story behind scripture.
