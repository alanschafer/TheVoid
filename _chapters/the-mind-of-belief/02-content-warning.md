This book contains frank discussions of religious trauma, psychological crisis, suicidal ideation, and the painful process of leaving high-control religious groups. While every effort has been made to provide accurate information and helpful resources, this book is not intended as a substitute for professional mental health care.

If you are experiencing thoughts of self-harm or are in psychological crisis, please seek immediate professional help or contact emergency services. Resources for crisis support are provided throughout this book.

The experiences described are based on the author's personal journey and extensive research into the psychology of belief. Individual experiences may vary significantly.

All identifying details of personal stories have been changed to protect privacy, except where individuals have given explicit permission to share their experiences.
