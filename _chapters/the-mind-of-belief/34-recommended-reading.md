- "Thinking, Fast and Slow" by Daniel Kahneman

- "The Righteous Mind" by Jonathan Haidt

- "Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion" by Robert Cialdini

**Religious Trauma and Recovery:**

- "Leaving the Fold" by Marlene Winell

- "Recovering Agency" by Luna Lindsey

- "The God Virus" by Darrel W. Ray

**Philosophy and Critical Thinking:**

- "Demon-Haunted World" by Carl Sagan

- "The Sense of Style" by Steven Pinker

- "How to Think" by Alan Jacobs

**Neuroscience and Consciousness:**

- "The Believing Brain" by Michael Shermer

- "Waking Up" by Sam Harris

- "The User Illusion" by Tor Nørretranders

**About the Author**

Alan Shaffer spent the first twenty-four years of his life as one of Jehovah's Witnesses before beginning a journey of deconstruction and recovery that led to complete intellectual and emotional liberation. He has since dedicated himself to understanding the psychology of belief systems and helping others navigate their own journeys from indoctrination to freedom.

His work combines personal experience with extensive research in neuroscience, psychology, and philosophy to provide both theoretical understanding and practical guidance for anyone seeking to live more consciously.

He lives with a deep appreciation for truth, beauty, and the ongoing adventure of conscious living.
