This book exists because of the courage of countless individuals who chose truth over comfort, often at tremendous personal cost. To the former Jehovah's Witnesses who shared their stories, offered support, and demonstrated that life after belief is not just possible but beautiful—thank you.

To the researchers and scientists whose work made this book possible: your dedication to understanding the human mind provides the foundation for genuine liberation. Special acknowledgment to the work of Daniel Kahneman, Jonathan Haidt, Robert Sapolsky, and the many neuroscientists whose research illuminates the mechanisms of belief.

To the therapists, counselors, and mental health professionals who specialize in religious trauma—your work saves lives and deserves far more recognition than it receives.

And finally, to my family—both the one I lost and the one I found. Love is stronger than doctrine, truth is more powerful than tradition, and authentic relationships are worth any sacrifice.

*"The mind is just as immeasurable as the vast universe."* — Lao Tzu
