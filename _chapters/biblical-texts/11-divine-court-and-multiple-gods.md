In the Septuagint at

(Deuteronomy 32:8,9 - )

We get a prime example of this

"When El Elyon divided the nations, when He separated the sons of Adam, He set the boundaries of the nations according to the number of the sons of God. But YHWH's portion is His people, Jacob is the lot of His inheritance."

So here it clearly indicates YHWH in the god of Jacob being Israel, not the Almighty God he's later claimed to be

Here it plainly states

"El Elyon is the most high" (Psalms 81)

And again at

(Psalms 82:6-7)

We can see very clearly the hierarchy

Plainly stated

"El Elyon stands in the divine assembly; among the gods He judges. He says: 'You are gods (plural elohim/theoi), Sons of the El Elyon (Most High). Yet you shall die like Adam (like mortals), and fall like one of the sarim (princes/archons).'"

So main many things that have been either purposeful or accidental changed this particular one has huge implications, in being some may not be worshiping the God they think they are, but through the Septuagint some scholars interpret this as one interpretive perspective behind today's modern misunderstanding

Scholarly Note: While some scholars argue that the Septuagint preserves earlier readings closer to those used in the time of Jesus,

Others maintain that the Masoretic Text reflects a careful preservation of Hebrew scripture within Jewish tradition.

Differences between the two are studied within the fields of textual criticism and historical linguistics.
