## Passages Interpreted as Depicting YHWH Engaging in Deception

Jeremiah 20:7-13:

"O LORD, thou hast misled me, and I was misled; thou art stronger than I, and hast prevailed." The prophet Jeremiah directly accuses God of manipulating and overpowering him through misleading information.
