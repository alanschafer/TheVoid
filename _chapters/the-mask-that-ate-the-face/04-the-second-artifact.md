Closes with the reminder that every sign, no matter how sacred, is only a doorway. Symbols point beyond themselves, but they cannot carry us through.

It is here that The Forbidden Path begins.

Where the artifacts whisper of hidden truths, the Path shows the cost of following them. What was hinted in fragments now unfolds as a story—of a people bound by law, of children who ask forbidden questions, and of the dangerous freedom that waits in the forest beyond the Ward.
