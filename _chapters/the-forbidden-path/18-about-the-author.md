Alan Shaffer writes psychologically rich speculative fiction and nonfiction exploring the edges of belief, control, and personal awakening. With a background in creative writing and lived experience navigating systems of thought, Shaffer’s work explores the emotional wreckage and quiet revelations that shape human identity.
