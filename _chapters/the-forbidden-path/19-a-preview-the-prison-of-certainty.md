For those who remember the world before the bars appeared.

Do you remember when you were very small, running around without a care?

There was nothing you were supposed to be doing, nothing you’d forgotten.

It hadn’t claimed you yet — that voice of “should” and “shouldn’t.”

No inner judge to blame you, no invisible weight pressing you to be busy.

Only the simple joy of being, before the prison door swung shut.
