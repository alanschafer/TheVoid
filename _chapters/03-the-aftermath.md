I never knew how right that older guy was.

## Two days after

I had finally gotten a generator. It was so shitty. It was louder inside the camper than outside, it moved around, and it only lasted about an hour. I packed up that night and suffered through it. The next night I couldn’t handle it. I went driving.

I was on a long road with many curves, to nowhere in particular. Everything was super fucked and I had nothing to do. I couldn’t sleep, because of the insomnia and because of the generator. I got to the point where I felt like my head was going to explode, and I was weeping bitterly, and I started screaming as I drove.

I then had an out-of-body experience: an aerial view of my own truck, following me from above as I drove.

After that, everything was different.

## The first year

The first year on the pipeline was really good, up until the incident. Then I could no longer sleep. I had insomnia constantly. I would oversleep for work.

I got worse progressively, but slowly. In 2020, my wife and I had our first child. Then I just worked for years.

## The quiet years

I worked at a commercial tire shop for three years.

I wasn’t thinking about the accident. I just moved on. But slowly, through those years, it drove itself back to me.

## The tape

Something drove me to get the tape of my statement. I couldn’t ignore it anymore. I went to St. Cloud and bought it for twenty dollars.

On the drive down, I was excited to hear my own words describe the incident.

I listened to the tape twice.

After I listened to the tape, I felt a shift in my mind.

I simulated in my mind what that night would have been like in a different light, with the fun filter removed and only the horror remaining. It was more than my mind could handle. I felt my mind break, and then my memory problems really started.

## The fall

Around the fall of 2023, things got severely worse. By January 2024, the memories were bringing me to the floor. My head would spin, I’d sweat, and I’d get flashes of memory I couldn’t recognize.
