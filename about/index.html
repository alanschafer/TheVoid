Some things deserve to stay.

The Void is an independent library for books, memories, and lived experience. A quiet place to read, return, and follow a thread.

The collection began with I’d Rather Not Say Goodbye, a memoir by Jamie Shaffer about a fatal crash and the years after it. It now also holds four works by Alan Shaffer on belief, evidence, and the mind: The Mind of Belief, Biblical Texts Under Critical Examination, The Bible in Human Time, and Psyche Paradox, and two works of fiction from the same author, from a set he calls the Artifacts: The Mask That Ate the Face and The Forbidden Path.

A library, not a feed.

Books have their own shelf. Individual sections have their own records. The archive connects them through titles, subjects, and full-text search.

There is no sign-up and no paywall. Start at the beginning, find a particular word, or save a section to return to later.

The words belong to their author.

Each book is presented as its author wrote it. The memoir is a personal account with a sources section. The nonfiction books argue for their own conclusions, and their views are the author’s. The two stories are allegories. Content notes sit at the start of each book.
