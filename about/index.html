Some things deserve to stay.

The Void is an independent library for books, memories, and lived experience. A quiet place to read, return, and follow a thread.

The collection begins with I’d Rather Not Say Goodbye, a memoir by Jamie Shaffer. It follows a fatal crash, the years after it, and a life that no longer fits a script.

A library, not a feed.

Books have their own shelf. Individual sections have their own records. The archive connects them through titles, subjects, and full-text search.

There is no sign-up and no paywall. Start at the beginning, find a particular word, or save a section to return to later.

The words belong to their author.

The memoir is presented as a personal account. The manuscript includes an opening based on public reporting, the author’s memories, a note where memory and the record differ, and a sources section.
